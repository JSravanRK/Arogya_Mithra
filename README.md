# 🩺 ArogyaMithra – Your Personal Healthcare Assistant

ArogyaMithra is a user-friendly and intelligent healthcare assistant app designed to simplify healthcare management for users and enhance collaboration between patients and healthcare providers. Built as a project for OOD&P (Object-Oriented Design & Programming), it offers multilingual support, AI-based symptom checking, appointment booking, video consultations, and secure access to health records.

---



## 🎯 Key Features

- 🔐 **User Authentication & Authorization** – Secure signup/login with role-based access (patient, doctor, admin).
- 🧠 **AI-Powered Symptom Checker** – Uses NLP and machine learning to recommend potential diagnoses and doctors.
- 📅 **Appointment Scheduling** – Clinic visits or video consultations with calendar integration.
- 👨‍⚕️ **Doctor Directory** – Filterable doctor database with reviews and specialties.
- 📹 **Secure Video Consultation** – Enables virtual check-ups via encrypted channels.
- 🧾 **Medical Reports & History** – Access, manage, and share health records and appointment logs.
- 🌐 **Multilingual Support** – Available in all official Indian languages to increase accessibility.
- 🛡️ **Data Privacy & Compliance** – Built with a strong focus on encrypted data handling and legal healthcare regulations.

---

## 📱 Target Users

- Young Adults (18–35) – General wellness and occasional consultations  
- Middle-aged Adults (36–55) – Chronic condition monitoring and follow-ups  
- Seniors (55+) – Regular appointments, health tracking, and document access
